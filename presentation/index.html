01 · Understanding the business

A product company with one clear promise: Value. Symmetry. Safety.

Thunder Mounts designs engineered mounting systems for garage door openers and door track — a modern replacement for the perforated angle iron installers have used for decades. The brand calls this the VSS™ Installation Standard.

What they sell

Opener mounts (TMS, TMX, TML), Marantec-style mounts (TMMRT), track mount sets (TMGT) in 12–29 in. lengths, plus selected openers, doors and Thunder Lights (in development).

Who buys

Garage door installers and door companies, contractors and builders, homeowners finishing a garage, and automotive enthusiasts who care how the space looks.

Brand promise

“Engineered for strength, clean aesthetics and reliable results.” The difference is visual and immediate — which makes photography and product clarity central to the website.

Sources: thundermounts.com homepage, product catalog and product pages (reviewed while preparing this proposal). Product names and list prices in the prototype reflect the public catalog.

02 · Current website experience

What we observed on thundermounts.com today

A fair, factual review of the live site. The current site communicates the core message and already sells online; the opportunities below are about depth, structure and control.

  • Built on Wix with a store, blog, members area and a mounting-instructions page. The homepage is a single long page led by a large text statement and installation photos.
  • Catalog of 19 listings under “All Products” with short descriptions (often one or two lines) and prices from $149.99 to $2,200. Some listings share the same photos; one door product shows $0.00 with a size option; one SKU label does not match its product name.
  • No dedicated dealer experience is publicly visible — no dealer pricing, application form or dealer ordering flow could be found without an account. (A generic members page exists.)
  • Navigation and content are page-based; sizing guidance, compatibility and “what’s in the box” are not presented consistently per product.
  • Strong assets to keep: the logo, the real installation photography, the before/after comparison and the “Call or text 805-338-6680” prominence.

We did not measure site performance or search rankings for this proposal and make no claims about them.

Payment providers (proposed)

Payments are processed through PayPal and/or Stripe using provider-hosted or tokenized card components. Card data is entered directly into the provider’s secure fields — the Thunder Mounts website never stores raw card numbers.

  • Contact, billing and shipping addresses with “same as shipping” shortcut
  • Shipping method selection with carrier rates in production
  • Discount codes, tax and shipping calculated server-side
  • Terms, privacy and return-policy links at the point of purchase
  • Order confirmation page and transactional email
  • Dealer accounts can be offered invoice / PO terms (configurable)

Prototype note: the demo checkout simulates the flow only — no live payments are taken. Live payment integration, tax and shipping rules are implemented during production development with the approved providers.

07 · Dealer portal

A professional account for the installers who sell Thunder Mounts

Application → review → approval

Dealers apply online with company details and resale documentation. Thunder Mounts reviews each application in the admin, assigns a pricing tier and approves or declines. The applicant sees a live status: Pending Approved Not approved

  • Dealer login with password recovery
  • Dealer catalog showing proposed tier pricing next to retail
  • Bulk order form and one-click reorder from history
  • Order history, tracking status, invoices & documents
  • Saved addresses, account contact, support and settings

Dealer pricing tiers, minimums and payment terms shown in the prototype are examples. In production they are fully configurable by Thunder Mounts and nothing is assumed about current dealer arrangements.

11 · Business benefits

What a unified system changes day to day

Operational advantages of running the storefront, dealer program, orders and content from one place. We do not project numerical improvements, rankings or revenue — these are the practical outcomes the system is designed to deliver.

Easier product updates

Change prices, photos, stock and specifications in minutes. Consistent product pages with the same sections every time — no more uneven listings.

Organized order handling

Every order moves through a visible workflow with payment status, tracking and notes — and customers are notified automatically at each step.

Better dealer access

Installers apply, get approved and reorder on their own schedule at the pricing Thunder Mounts sets — reducing phone and email back-and-forth.

Improved content control

Pages, menus, blog posts, galleries, reviews and SEO fields are all editable by the owner or a designated editor with role-based access.

A consistent customer experience

One design system across storefront, dealer portal and checkout on desktop, tablet and mobile — with the real Thunder Mounts identity throughout.

Security & ownership

Provider-hosted payments, encrypted authentication, audit logs and backups. Thunder Mounts owns its content, product data and customer relationships.

Appendix · Technical architecture & hosting

A maintainable full-stack foundation

The prototype is static HTML/CSS/JS for review on GitHub Pages. The production system requires a server, database and secure services. A final hosting recommendation will be made after confirming compatibility with Thunder Mounts’ current domain, hosting and commerce setup — a static host cannot run the production system.

Presentation

  • Responsive storefront & dealer portal
  • Server-rendered pages for SEO
  • Design system from this prototype
  • Accessible, mobile-first

Application

  • Secure admin & dealer authentication
  • Role-based permissions & audit log
  • Catalog, pricing rules, inventory
  • Orders, fulfillment, notifications
  • CMS content & media API

Data

  • Relational database (products, orders, customers, dealers, content)
  • Object storage for media & documents
  • Automated backups & restore
  • Export of customer / order data

Integrations

  • PayPal / Stripe (tokenized, PCI handled by provider)
  • Shipping & tracking API
  • SMTP transactional email
  • GA4, Search Console, Merchant Center
  • Secrets stored server-side only

Security

HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, optional 2FA for admins, rate limiting, dependency updates, no raw card data on the server.

Hosting (to confirm)

Managed cloud hosting with staging and production environments, monitored uptime and scheduled backups. Domain and DNS remain under Thunder Mounts’ control.

Handover

Admin training, documentation, and a production feature checklist (see documentation folder) so the team can run the site independently.
